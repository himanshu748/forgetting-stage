# Brand

`icon-primary.png` is the pick. Bold flat shapes, no thin linework, so it survives
being scaled to 48px. Curtains parting inside a playbill frame, the fabric falling away
as fragments. Reads as theatre instantly and as loss on a second look.

Alternates kept for reference:
- `alt-arch-ornate.png` and `alt-arch-curtains.png`: right concept, too much fold
  detail. They look good at 1024 and turn to mush small.

## What was rejected and why

The first attempt was a pin through a paper card. It failed because it drew the
**mechanic** instead of a **symbol**. A pin and card is what the game does, but with no
context it reads as a price tag, the fragments look like party confetti, and nothing in
it says theatre. An icon has a quarter second to communicate.

## If iterating

The curtain shapes are slightly claw-like and the fragments read a little like teeth.
Worth one more pass with softer curtain edges. Palette is correct and should not change:
`#0B0B0B` ground, `#F2EDE4` ink, `#FF3B1F` accent.

Needs a 1024x1024 export for the Shipaton submission, which this already satisfies.
